Introduction

This policy explains how Sekreter Büro Tercüme ve Ticaret Ltd. Şti. ("Sekreter Büro", "we") processes personal data and cookies when you visit our website (sekreterburo.com), under Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data ("KVKK") and, where applicable, the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR").

Data Controller

Under KVKK, the data controller is Sekreter Büro Tercüme ve Ticaret Ltd. Şti. You can reach us using the contact details at the end of this page.

Cookies and Data We Collect

Our website currently collects only anonymous/statistical visitor data via Google Analytics (GA4) — page views, session duration, approximate geographic location, and device/browser information. This data is never collected without the consent described below. We do not currently use any other third-party tracking tool, advertising cookie, or strictly necessary (technical) cookie — our contact form processes what you submit directly, without setting any cookie.

Why We Use Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors use our site and improve our services. This data is not used for personalized advertising.

Legal Basis

We only enable analytics cookies where you've given explicit consent under KVKK Art. 5, via the cookie consent banner on our site. For visitors under GDPR, the legal basis is explicit consent under GDPR Art. 6(1)(a). If you don't consent, or withdraw consent, analytics cookies are disabled, and we can request deletion of any previously collected data via our Google Analytics settings.

International Data Transfer

Google Analytics is provided by Google LLC, which processes data on servers located in the United States. This means your data is transferred internationally once you consent. Under KVKK's rules on international data transfer, this transfer is carried out solely on the basis of your explicit consent; no data is sent to Google unless you consent.

Retention Period

Google Analytics cookies (e.g. _ga, _ga_*) are typically stored in your browser for up to 2 years, depending on Google's standard Analytics settings. The local storage record where we save your cookie preference ("accepted" or "rejected") is kept in your browser until you change it.

Your Rights

Under KVKK Art. 11, you have the right to learn whether we process your personal data, request information about it, learn its purpose and whether it's used accordingly, know the third parties to whom it's transferred domestically or abroad, request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data, request its deletion once the grounds for processing no longer apply, and request that these actions be notified to third parties to whom your data has been transferred. Visitors under GDPR additionally have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection. You can send us these requests using the contact details below.

Managing Your Preferences

You can change your analytics cookie preference at any time. Click "Cookie Settings" in the site footer to update your preferences or withdraw your consent.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. For significant changes, the consent banner may be shown again to re-obtain your cookie consent.
