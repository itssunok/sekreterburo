About Us /əˈbaʊt ʌs/

Two Generations,
One Passion

Sekreter Büro was founded in 1980 by ER OK, a professional English translator who identified a gap in Turkey's translation landscape. Today, under second-generation leadership, the company carries its founder's values forward.

"For forty years, we have spoken our clients' language."

— ER OK, Founder, 1980

With our team of expert translators, we handle legal, financial, medical, technical, commercial and patent translations, as well as your personal documents, with the same care and attention every time. We approach every project with the same professionalism, delivering full commitment to confidentiality, high quality standards and on-time delivery, and with over 40 years of experience we remain your address for accurate, reliable translation in nearly 100 languages.

Our Services /aʊr ˈsɜːrvɪsɪz/

Translation Solutions
Tailored to Your Needs

From corporate projects to personal applications, and from oral interpreting services to sworn and notary-approved document processing, we offer professional solutions for all your translation needs. Send us your document by email and our expert team will review it and provide you with a quote in a short time.

⚖️

Legal Translation

Contracts, court rulings, powers of attorney, official documents and other legal texts are carefully translated by our experienced sworn translators, in line with legal terminology, accurately, completely and under strict confidentiality.

🏥

Medical Translation

Medical reports, drug leaflets, clinical research documents, patient files and other health-related documents are translated by our expert translators with command of medical terminology, upholding accuracy, confidentiality and quality standards. Translations are delivered with sworn translator certification when required.

⚙️

Technical & Specification Translation

Technical documents, product specifications, user manuals, engineering documents and industrial documentation are translated accurately and completely by our expert translators, in line with technical terminology.

💼

Commercial & Financial Translation

Business correspondence, financial statements, financial reports, investment documents, commercial contracts and other corporate documents are translated accurately, consistently and under strict confidentiality by our expert translators, in line with commercial and financial terminology.

💡

Patent Translation

Patent applications, invention descriptions, claims and intellectual property documents are translated accurately, completely and under strict confidentiality by our expert translators, who are well versed in technical and legal terminology.

🌐

Website Translation

Your website, e-commerce platform and digital content are professionally localized to match the cultural context, language use and expectations of your target audience — delivering not just an accurate translation, but natural, effective content suited to the target market.

📚

Literary Translation

Novels, short stories, poetry, essays and other literary works are translated with care, preserving the work's style, expressive power and the author's original voice. Our aim is to carry not only the meaning of the text, but also its emotional and aesthetic integrity, as naturally as possible into the target language.

🎓

Diploma & Transcript

Certified translation of diplomas and transcripts by our sworn translators, for international education or job applications.

✈️

Visa Documents

Accurate and timely translation of all personal and official documents required for visa applications, by our sworn translators.

🪪

Passport & ID

Valid translation of passports, national IDs and other identity documents for official authorities.

📜

Notary and Apostille Services

Notarization, Ministry of Foreign Affairs approval and apostille processing for documents prepared by our sworn translators.

💍

Civil Status Documents

Official translation of marriage certificates, divorce decrees, birth certificates and civil status documents.

🏢

Job Application Documents

Professional translation of CVs, reference letters and all documents needed for international job applications.

🎧

Simultaneous Interpretation

For large meetings and high-level conferences — the interpreter works in real time from a booth, rendering the speaker's words into the target language as they are spoken.

🤫

Whisper Interpretation

Real-time interpretation whispered to a single listener seated beside the interpreter within a group setting.

🎤

Consecutive Interpretation

The interpreter stands beside the speaker and re-delivers paragraphs in the target language at natural pauses.

📞

Phone Interpretation

Enables a professional phone conversation with any party via a conference call mediated by the interpreter.

🤝

Escort Interpreter

An interpreter who accompanies your international guests throughout their visit, covering all meetings and social engagements.

Our Values /aʊr ˈvæljuːz/

Your Documents
Belong Only to You

With over 40 years of experience, we serve every client with the same care and professionalism. The core values that make us one of Ankara's most established and trusted translation offices are our commitment to experience, confidentiality, trust and quality.

🏛️

Established Experience

Drawing on the knowledge we've built since 1980 and our long-standing client relationships, we serve as a trusted solution partner for legal, commercial, technical and personal translation needs.

🔐

Confidentiality & Limited Access

Your documents and personal information are protected under our confidentiality principles. Only assigned translators and authorized office staff can access your files — no information or document is ever shared with third parties.

📁

Secure Document Delivery

You can safely send us your documents by email, online communication channels, courier or post. Your information is carefully protected and securely managed throughout the entire process.

✅

Quality Assurance

Every translation is approved by our team and translators; notarization and apostille services are available when needed.
